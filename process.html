Process

How we work with you and your brand.

We're a production company, but on most projects we work more like your agency. You tell us what you're going for, and we bring in the right people to make it — creatives whose style already fits your brand, wherever in the world they happen to be. Nothing gets run through a template.

  1. Purpose
  2. Shape
  3. Forge
  4. Refine
  5. Ship

1 / 5

Purpose

We start with a call. Before anything else, we want to understand what this is actually for, whether that's selling something, telling a story, or filling a room. Once we know, it shapes every decision we make after. And it's specific to you. We're not running you through the same playbook as the last client.

2 / 5

Shape

Once we know the goal, we put the team together. This is the part people don't expect from a production company: we hand-pick the creatives whose work already fits your brand, and they can be anywhere in the world. Then we plan it out with you, from the look and the story down to the shot list and how the day runs. You see all of it before we shoot a thing.

Concept / mood board media/process/step-02.jpg

3 / 5

Forge

Then we shoot, wherever it needs to happen. The people we brought in for you show up and do the work. They're good at what they do and easy to have on set. What matters to us is that your brand feels like it's in the right hands, not handed to whoever happened to be free that week.

On set / BTS media/process/step-03.jpg

4 / 5

Refine

Editing, sound, color. This is where it comes together. We keep at it until it feels right for you specifically, not just good enough to pass. We won't send it over until it's something you'd be glad to put in front of your CMO or your client without thinking twice.

Edit suite / color media/process/step-04.jpg

5 / 5

Ship

You get the final cuts in whatever formats you need, ready to go. And we don't disappear the moment it's delivered. If you want help getting it in front of people, or you're already thinking about the next one, we're around. We'd rather be the team you keep calling than a one-off.

So bring us your brand and what you're trying to do. We'll put the right people around it and make something that feels like yours. Let's get on a call.
