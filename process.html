Process

How we work with your brand.

Figure out the why before anyone picks up a camera.

  1. Purpose
  2. Shape

1 / 5

Purpose

First we talk about why this piece exists. Launch something new? Tell your origin story? Give people a reason to care? That purpose becomes the compass — every decision we make comes back to it. Picture your audience walking away knowing exactly why your brand matters.

Discovery call / kickoff media/process/step-01.jpg

2 / 5

Shape

You bring the brand. We bring ideas. Mood, story, shot list — we sketch what this could look and feel like before anyone rolls camera. Spotlights, narratives, immersions — whatever lane fits, you'll see it on paper first so shoot day isn't a guessing game.

Concept / mood board media/process/step-02.jpg

3 / 5

Forge

This is the fun part. Cameras roll, talent shows up, the plan meets reality. We run tight sets with good people and zero ego — professional, respectful, and built to capture what we mapped out. You're building the raw material for something you're proud to put your name on.

On set / BTS media/process/step-03.jpg

4 / 5

Refine

Edit, sound, color, polish — the part that makes it feel like yours. Our in-house team and trusted collaborators push until it lands. We don't ship until you'd happily send it to your mom (or your CMO — same energy).

Edit suite / color media/process/step-04.jpg

5 / 5

Ship

Final cuts, every format you need, delivered clean. Already have a launch plan? Love it. Need help getting eyes on the work? We can connect you with people who live in distribution — or help you figure it out. Either way, the piece does what we set out to do in step one.

We'll make something you're proud of and have a good time doing it. Can't wait to hear what you're building.
